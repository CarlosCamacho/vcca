# VCCA privacy policy

*Last updated: October 10, 2026*

VCCA, the Tandy Color Computer 3 emulator for Android by Carlos A. Camacho,
does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.** VCCA contains no advertising,
  analytics or crash-reporting code, and no third-party SDKs.
- **No network access of its own.** The emulator and its interface run from
  files inside the app. VCCA does not request Android's Internet permission.
- **Your files stay on your device.** ROMs, disk and tape images, hard drive
  images, save states, screenshots, videos and settings are stored only on
  your device, in the app's own storage or wherever you choose to save them.
  VCCA reads a file only when you pick it.
- **Links open in your browser.** Links in Help and About, and **Report a
  bug**, open in your web browser. Report a bug opens a GitHub form with your
  VCCA version and device model filled in; nothing is sent unless you submit
  that form yourself, and what you submit is governed by
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Permissions.** VCCA asks only for vibration, for touch feedback on the
  on-screen keyboard and joystick.

Uninstalling VCCA removes everything it stored in its own storage.

Questions: open an issue at
[github.com/CarlosCamacho/vcca/issues](https://github.com/CarlosCamacho/vcca/issues).
